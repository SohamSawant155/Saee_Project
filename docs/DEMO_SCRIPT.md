# Demo Script

## 1. Opening

Introduce Laksh MedChain as a drug inventory and supply chain tracking system for SIH1627. State the objective: improve availability, procurement efficiency, real-time tracking, decision-making, security, and governance.

## 2. Command Center

Start on the Overview screen.

- Show available units, action items, cold-chain alerts, and service level.
- Explain that the dashboard gives a government or program manager a live operating view.
- Point to demand trend and decision alerts.
- Open an AI replenishment recommendation and queue it into procurement.

## 3. Inventory

Open Inventory.

- Search for an antibiotic or vaccine.
- Filter by Critical or Cold Chain Alert.
- Explain batch-level tracking: quantity, reserved stock, vendor, expiry, location, temperature, and status.
- Add a medicine to the cart to show procurement flow.

## 4. Store

Open Store.

- Show verified supplier cards.
- Compare available units, lead time, price, and QA score.
- Edit purchase order quantity.
- Explain invoice subtotal, GST estimate, and PO value.

## 5. Orders

Open Orders.

- Select an in-transit order.
- Show buyer, seller, ETA, value, source, destination, and timeline.
- Explain how vendors, distributors, and hospitals update the same order lifecycle.

## 6. Forecasting

Open Forecasting.

- Show predicted demand vs actual demand.
- Explain demand drivers: outbreak signal, historical consumption, supplier lead-time volatility, and expiry redistribution.
- Explain how this reduces stockouts and wastage.

## 7. Compliance

Open Compliance.

- Show license registry, module coverage, and audit trail.
- Explain RBAC, license verification, batch traceability, and cold-chain deviation handling.

## 8. Delivery Plan

Open Delivery Plan.

- Show sprint roadmap and risks.
- Explain how the MVP grows into a production backend with APIs, PostgreSQL, telemetry, and forecasting service.

## 9. Closing

Close with measurable impact:

- Better access to essential medicines.
- Lower wastage and procurement cost.
- Real-time vendor accountability.
- Data-driven policy decisions.
- Audit-ready and secure supply chain operations.
